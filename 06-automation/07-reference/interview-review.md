# Automation Interview Review

Use these prompts after completing a practical project.

1. Which scenarios did you automate, and why?
2. Which checks did you place below the UI?
3. How do you design stable locators?
4. How does your selected tool synchronize with a dynamic page?
5. How do you isolate tests and their data?
6. What does your framework abstract, and what stays visible in tests?
7. How do you investigate a failure that passes on retry?
8. Which evidence does CI preserve?
9. What would make you select a different tool?
10. Which automation work would you intentionally avoid?

Answer with a definition, a real example, a decision or trade-off, and an outcome. Do not claim a tool or result you cannot explain in detail.
