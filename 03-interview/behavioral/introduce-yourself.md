# Behavioral Interview Learning Guide

Use these notes to build honest, specific answers from your own experience. Do not memorize every sentence.

## Recommended order

1. [Professional self-introduction](self-introduction.md) — prepare 30-second and 90-second versions.
2. [Common HR questions](common-hr-questions.md) — strengths, weaknesses, motivation, and project stories.
3. [QA role and responsibilities](qa-role-and-responsibilities.md) — explain your daily work and senior responsibilities.
4. [Complete interview notes](complete-interview-notes.md) — deeper Notion-derived reference and final revision.

## Practice rule

For each answer, give a real context, your action, the result, and what you learned. Replace generic claims with one believable example.
