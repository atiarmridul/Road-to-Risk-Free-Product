# Professional Self-Introduction

> **সহজভাবে / In simple words:** A self-introduction is a short story about who you are, what you do, and why your experience fits the role.
>
> **কেন গুরুত্বপূর্ণ / Why it matters:** A clear introduction creates context for the rest of the interview.


## 90-second professional introduction

Assalamu Alaikum. I’m **Md. Atiar Rahman Chowdhury**, an **ISTQB® Certified Software QA Engineer** with more than six years of experience testing web and mobile applications.

My work covers the full QA lifecycle: understanding requirements, finding risks and edge cases, designing test cases, executing tests, reporting defects, and validating releases. I have experience with functional, integration, regression, exploratory, UI/UX, API, and performance testing. I also investigate failures by checking API responses and application logs.

For automation, I have worked with Selenium and Java, WebdriverIO, Appium, and REST Assured, and I am continuing to build my Playwright and k6 skills. I collaborate with developers, designers, product managers, and other stakeholders in Agile teams. I also support junior team members and communicate test progress and release risks clearly.

I’m now looking for a role where I can use this experience to help a team release reliable, user-friendly software while continuing to grow in automation and quality engineering.

## 30-second version

I’m Md. Atiar Rahman Chowdhury, an ISTQB-certified QA engineer with more than six years of web and mobile testing experience. I work across requirements, test design, API and UI testing, defect investigation, automation, and release validation. My main strength is combining a user’s point of view with technical investigation, and I’m looking for an opportunity to help a team deliver reliable products while growing further in automation.

## How to personalize it before an interview

- Confirm that your current employer, job title, and years of experience are still correct.
- Mention only tools you can explain or demonstrate confidently.
- Add one measurable result, such as reduced regression time or escaped defects, if you can verify it.
- Match the final sentence to the job description.
- Practice aloud; aim for 60–90 seconds without rushing.
