### 👨‍💻 Professional Introduction
#### 🎤 2-Minute Professional Introduction
**Assalamu Alaikum,**
I’m Md. Atiar Rahman Chowdhury, an ISTQB Certified Software QA Engineer with over 6 years of experience in testing web and mobile applications. Currently, I work as a Software QA Engineer (Automation) at [Portonics Limited](https://portonics.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com), where I contribute to a leading telecom application in Bangladesh serving a monthly active user of 22.5 million.
My focus is on ensuring product quality by giving confidence that the application is usable, stable, reliable, and ready for end users through effective manual and automation testing practices.
My responsibilities include requirement analysis, test planning, test case writing, test execution, bug reporting, and preparing testing reports to ensure software quality throughout the development process. I work closely with developers, designers, product managers, and stakeholders in Agile environments to ensure better collaboration and smooth software delivery. 
I have experience in both manual and automation testing, including functional, regression, UI/UX, integration, and performance testing. I also have hands-on experience testing Android and iOS applications.
For automation, I have worked with tools like **Selenium, WebdriverIO, Appium, and Playwright**. I transitioned to WebdriverIO for efficient asynchronous test execution. Additionally, I leverage Appium for mobile application automation
I collaborate closely with developers to identify root causes and prioritize bug fixes based on impact and severity by troubleshooting bugs through analyzing Kibana logs and API responses, enabling quick identification and resolution of issues.
Besides testing, I also mentor junior QA engineers and continuously improve my technical and automation skills.
Furthermore, I manage and deliver detailed reports for stakeholders, covering status updates, pre-and post-deployment reports, defect tracking, and overall testing progress.
I look forward to exploring how my experience and technical expertise can add value to your organization.
