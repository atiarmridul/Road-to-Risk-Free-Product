# Jira for QA Engineers

> **সহজভাবে / In simple words:** Jira is a work-tracking tool used to record stories, tasks, defects, and their progress.
>
> **কেন গুরুত্বপূর্ণ / Why it matters:** QA uses it to connect testing evidence, product risk, ownership, and release status.


Jira supports work tracking; it does not replace test thinking or communication.

## What QA commonly manages

- Stories and acceptance-criteria clarification
- Test tasks and execution dependencies
- Defects, evidence, severity, priority, and affected version
- Links between requirements, tests, defects, and releases
- Filters and dashboards for triage and release status

## Strong defect ticket

Include a specific title, environment/build, preconditions, minimal reproduction steps, expected and actual behavior, frequency, evidence, impact, and relevant logs or requests. Avoid sensitive data.

## Workflow considerations

A workflow should make ownership and next action clear. Typical states include open, selected, in progress, ready for QA, reopened, resolved, and closed. Exact states vary; understand the transition rules and definition of done used by the team.

## Interview questions

### How do you use Jira during a sprint?

I review scope and dependencies, clarify acceptance criteria, track test work, link defects to the affected story and build, support triage with evidence, and use filters or dashboards to communicate risk and readiness.

### What makes a Jira dashboard useful?

It answers a decision question. For release readiness, I would show critical scope status, unresolved defects by risk, execution trend, blocked work, reopen or leakage signals, and ownership—not just raw ticket counts.
